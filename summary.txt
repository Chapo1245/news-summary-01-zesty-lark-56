World news, Wednesday 11 November 2026: Two days of US-mediated talks between Russia and Ukraine in Abu Dhabi ended with agreement on the largest prisoner exchange of the war, but no ceasefire and no deal on territory (Reuters). US envoys Jared Kushner and Steve Witkoff said they would carry the talks to Moscow the next day. In the Strait of Hormuz, Iran said an oil tanker caught fire after hitting a naval mine, a day after its Revolutionary Guards said three tankers had burned; Britain's UKMTO confirmed an incident, though Reuters could not verify the mine claim. US Secretary of State Marco Rubio said in Paris that economic pressure on Iran was working and that major combat operations were no longer needed. Hoax bomb threats forced evacuations at hundreds of schools in Italy after about 6,300 received threatening emails, with hundreds more schools in Britain and Ireland also targeted; no explosives were found and police treated the messages as hoaxes (Reuters). At the COP31 climate summit in Antalya, UN chief Antonio Guterres told more than 70 world leaders that fossil fuels "must go the way of the dinosaurs" and urged taxes on oil companies' windfall profits, noting the nine largest earned $95 billion in one quarter. In Spain, unions said four million workers joined the first general strike in 14 years over housing costs, with average rent at 1,176 euros a month, ahead of a snap election on 29 November (Reuters). Russia's ambassador to the United States, Alexander Darchiev, died suddenly in Moscow aged 66, his ministry said. Kenya discharged its last Ebola patient and began a 42-day countdown to being declared Ebola-free, while the outbreak in DR Congo reached 11,042 cases and 5,387 deaths (Reuters). In Washington, the House voted 211-204 to send an impeachment resolution against Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth to committee, effectively killing it.
